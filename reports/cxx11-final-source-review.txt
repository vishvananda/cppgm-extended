C++11 suite source review (final profile)

These exact source-SHA dispositions are applied before either timed compiler role.
An exclusion is not a pass. Edits to a pinned source invalidate its disposition.
The frozen original4092 GCC configurations and815 Clang configurations, original
compiler outcomes, failed prototypes and full sources remain separately recorded.
The declared CI profile has its own denominator and never rewrites those results.

The required rule comes from published wording, adopted decisions and the course
contract. Diagnostic wording, count, severity and recovery are not correctness
requirements. A normal diagnostic suffices; crashes, compiler-internal errors,
timeouts and linking failures cannot satisfy a negative compilation oracle.

Coverage changes from the original GCC cohort:
  62 exact sources /124 configurations excluded from BOTH timed compiler roles.
  One valid source /2 configurations retains acceptance through standard #line
  projection. Original and projected hashes are frozen; the same staged file is
  used for both compilers, with staging excluded from both elapsed measurements.
  Two sources /4 configurations change obsolete successful-object expectations
  to required ordinary diagnostics, based on independent whole-TU rule analysis.
  3968 configurations /1984 sources remain selected from GCC cpp0x.
  The complete Clang815 manifest remains byte-identical to the prior cohort.

Neither profile claims the complete upstream suite, all GNU extensions, original
Clang FileCheck/IR/AST/ABI oracles, or a mathematical proof of all C++ behavior.
Open/drafting questions remain explicit coverage limitations; proposals are not
implemented to imitate a native compiler. The selected corpus tests acceptance,
required rejection, static assertions and the declared GCC runtime outcomes.

The confirmed compiler repairs retain required earliest-owning regressions.
Unused-template NDR decisions do not waive actually instantiated constraints:
bounded active-demand checks exposed and drove the B6 friend-pack and TT-head
repairs, plus dependent noexcept equivalence and known-true compatibility.
No experimental friend-template namespace-owner change was accepted: its lookup
premise was not established. Its exact source is a standards-review limitation.

The offline catalog includes complete pinned issue texts and necessary adopted
papers; background-only entries explicitly do not assert implementation/adoption.
