Fifth validated fix block: GNU frame-address builtin
--------------------------------------------------
__builtin_frame_address uses the builtin registry and typed intrinsic facts.
A frontend-neutral LowIR frame_address operation reads the physical x86-64
activation frame; a constant nonzero level follows saved frame pointers with
ordinary untyped pointer loads. A live operation reserves RBP and prevents
frame-pointer omission. Inline calls observe their containing activation.
No proxy local address or stack-alignment workaround substitutes for a frame.
