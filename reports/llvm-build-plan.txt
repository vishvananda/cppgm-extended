Result-storage candidate, B38, 2026-11-09
----------------------------------------
This four-part group builds on accepted B33 (7a7579f79):
1. Source indirect class results carry alias=exclusive_result as a separate
   bounded fact. Memory forwarding reuses source-field loads across writes
   to that result. Other pointer roots and out-of-extent writes stay aliases.
2. Copy coalescing consumes the same bounded fact to combine adjacent field
   transfers and forward temporary copies. Ordinary overlapping pointers
