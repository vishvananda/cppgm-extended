representable values. The fallback evaluated a dense value for the whole
array, hit its storage/step budget, and expanded the implicit tail as runtime
stores. The required PA16:300 1 GiB pointer-array control fails on the parent
with bad_alloc under the 512 MiB frontend cap and passes on the candidate.

and constant-initialization requirements; no later CWG decision is involved.

class contexts uncached. Integral expressions already evaluated by the old
path are not probed again. The containing array stays a prefix plus zero range.
