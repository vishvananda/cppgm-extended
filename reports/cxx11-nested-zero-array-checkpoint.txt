Nested/default and implicit-zero array checkpoint (2026-11-06)

Compiler commit 269dbf2ad closes CXX11-023 and CXX11-024. The combined reference
family remains open for CXX11-025; this is not completion of the campaign.

literal-class constexpr defaults are retained in every dimension. CXX11-024
constructs only actual prefix elements, memoizes proven aggregate zero defaults,
and projects implicit-zero subobjects through typed layout. The located value

pointers as well as compile-time leaves. A spare-allocation-only intermediate
still hit the two-second CPU limit on a 29-level shared type graph; the complete
fix also bounds that repeated type work. The failed intermediate is retained.
