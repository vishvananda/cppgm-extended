1405. constexpr and mutable members of literal types
Section: 6.9 [basic.types [1]] Status: CD3 Submitter: Richard Smith Date: 2011-10-21

[Moved to DR at the April, 2013 meeting.]

Currently, literal class types can have mutable members. It is not clear whether that
poses any particular problems with constexpr objects and constant expressions, and if
so, what should be done about it.

Proposed resolution (February, 2012):

Change 7.7 [expr.const [2]] paragraph 2 as follows:

-

...

-

an lvalue-to-rvalue conversion (7.3.2 [conv.lval [3]]) unless it is applied to

-

a glvalue of integral or enumeration type that refers to a non-volatile const object
with a preceding initialization, initialized with a constant expression, or

-

a glvalue of literal type that refers to a non-volatile object defined with constexpr,
or that refers to a [INSERT: non-mutable] sub-object of such an object, or

-

a glvalue of literal type that refers to a non-volatile temporary object whose lifetime
has not ended, initialized with a constant expression;

-

...
