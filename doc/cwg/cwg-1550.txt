1550. Parenthesized throw-expression operand of conditional-expression
Section: 7.6.16 [expr.cond [1]] Status: CD3 Submitter: Nikolay Ivchenkov Date:
2012-09-04

[Moved to DR at the April, 2013 meeting.]

The current wording of 7.6.16 [expr.cond [2]] paragraph 2 says,

If either the second or the third operand has type void, then the lvalue-to-rvalue
(7.3.2 [conv.lval [3]]), array-to-pointer (7.3.3 [conv.array [4]]), and
function-to-pointer (7.3.4 [conv.func [5]]) standard conversions are performed on the
second and third operands, and one of the following shall hold:

-

The second or the third operand (but not both) is a throw-expression (14.2 [except.throw
[6]]); the result is of the type of the other and is a prvalue.

-

Both the second and the third operands have type void; the result is of type void and is
a prvalue. [Note: This includes the case where both operands are throw-expressions. —end
note]

A parenthesized throw-expression is a primary-expression, not a throw-expression. Should
a parenthesized throw-expression be considered a throw-expression for this purpose?

Proposed resolution (October, 2012):

Change 7.6.16 [expr.cond [2]] paragraph 2 as follows:

If either the second or the third operand has type void, [DELETE: then the
lvalue-to-rvalue (7.3.2 [conv.lval [3]]), array-to-pointer (7.3.3 [conv.array [4]]), and
function-to-pointer (7.3.4 [conv.func [5]]) standard conversions are performed on the
second and third operands, and] one of the following shall hold:

-

The second or the third operand (but not both) is a [INSERT: (possibly parenthesized)]
throw-expression (14.2 [except.throw [6]]); the result is of the type [INSERT: and value
category] of the other [DELETE: and is a prvalue].

-
