Wording for guaranteed copy elision through simplified value categories
This paper provides wording for P0135R0 [1].

This also resolves the following core issues:
1565 [2]: Copy elision and lifetime of initializer_list underlying array
1590 [3]: Bypassing non-copy/move constructor copying
1599 [4]: Lifetime of initializer_list underlying array
1697 [5]: Lifetime extension and copy elision
2022 [6] [PARTIAL]: Copy elision in constant expressions (except for NRVO)
