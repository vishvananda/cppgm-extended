2346. Local variables in default arguments
Section: 9.3.4.7 [dcl.fct.default [1]] Status: CD5 Submitter: Geoffrey Romer Date:
2017-04-26

[Adopted as a DR as part of paper P0588R1 at the October, 2018 meeting.]

According to 9.3.4.7 [dcl.fct.default [2]] paragraph 7,

A local variable shall not appear as a potentially-evaluated expression in a default
argument.

This prohibits plausible uses of constexpr and static local variables. Presumably this
rule should be similar to the one in 11.6 [class.local [3]] paragraph 1, regarding local
classes, which applies to odr-use, not potential evaluation, and to variables with
automatic storage duration.
