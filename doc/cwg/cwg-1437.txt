1437. alignas in alias-declaration
Section: 9.2.4 [dcl.typedef [1]] Status: CD3 Submitter: Daveed Vandevoorde Date:
2012-01-02

[Moved to DR at the April, 2013 meeting.]

Consider the following:


    struct S { int i; };
    using A alignas(alignof(long long)) = S;


9.13.2 [dcl.align [2]] paragraph 1 allows an alignment-specifier to be applied to the
declaration of a class or enumeration type, which A arguably is. The specification
should be clarified to indicate that such a usage is not permitted, however.

Proposed resolution (October, 2012):

Change 9.13.2 [dcl.align [2]] paragraph 1 as follows:

An alignment-specifier may be applied to a variable or to a class data member, but it
shall not be applied to a bit-field, a function parameter, [DELETE: the formal parameter
of a catch clause] [INSERT: an exception-declaration] (14.4 [except.handle [3]]), or a
variable declared with the register storage class specifier. An alignment-specifier may
also be applied to the declaration [DELETE: of a class or enumeration type] [INSERT: or
definition of a class (in an elaborated-type-specifier (9.2.9.5 [dcl.type.elab [4]]) or
class-head ( Clause 11 [class [5]]), respectively) and to the declaration or definition
of an enumeration (in an opaque-enum-declaration or enum-head, respectively (9.8.1
[dcl.enum [6]]))]. An alignment-specifier with an ellipsis is a pack expansion (13.7.4
[temp.variadic [7]]).
