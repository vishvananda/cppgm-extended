2101. Incorrect description of type- and value-dependence
Section: 13.8.3 [temp.dep [1]] Status: CD4 Submitter: Maxim Kartashev Date: 2015-03-16

[Moved to DR at the October, 2015 meeting.]

According to 13.8.3 [temp.dep [2]] paragraph 1,

Expressions may be type-dependent (on the type of a template parameter) or
value-dependent (on the value of a non-type template parameter).

Proposed resolution (May, 2015):

Change 13.8.3 [temp.dep [2]] paragraph 1 to read,

An expression may be type-dependent (that is, its type may depend on a template
parameter) or value-dependent (that is, its value when evaluated as a constant
expression (7.7 [expr.const [3]]) may depend on a template parameter) as described in
this subclause."
